Gardendoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

This policy explains what information Gardendoku (the "game", published by TonTech) collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices you have.

1. The short version

  • No sign-up. No email, phone number, name or password is needed, and puzzles work offline.
  • The game never collects your location, contacts, photos or microphone audio.
  • Gameplay statistics go to TonTech's own server, not to a third-party analytics SDK.
  • The weekly league uses a random identifier created on your device. It is not tied to your name.
  • The game shows ads. On iPhone, tracking only happens if you allow it.

2. Who we are

TonTech publishes Gardendoku and is responsible for your information. Contact: info@tontech.co (section 11).

3. Information we collect

Anonymous gameplay analytics

The game sends gameplay events to our own server (gardendoku-api.tontech.co): for example sessions, puzzles started, solved or abandoned, hints used, ads shown and purchases (product and price). Each event carries a random identifier generated on your device, the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the language setting. We use them to balance puzzles, find bugs and understand which features players enjoy. They are not sold and not used for advertising. We do not use any third-party analytics SDK.

Weekly league

To place you in the weekly league, the game uses a device-generated identifier and your puzzle results. There is no account and no player name.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. Your purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account with the store, which is how they can be restored.

Crash reports (optional)

If enabled, the game may send a crash report to our crash reporting provider, Sentry: technical details such as the device model, OS version, app version and the error.

Location

The game never collects your location.

4. Ads, consent and install attribution

  • The game shows ads served through NUVIAD, TonTech's advertising partner. NUVIAD receives only a country derived from your IP address, never your precise location.
  • When enabled, the game also shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob is subject to Google's policy and may collect your advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions. Where the law requires it, the in-app consent prompt asks you first.
  • To measure which ad campaigns led to an install, the game sends an install report to NUVIAD (notify.nuviad.com). Apple's SKAdNetwork also reports installs and a few in-game milestones to ad networks without any identifier.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the game asks for your permission before tracking. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game: the weekly league and delivering purchases.
  • To improve the game: gameplay statistics and crash reports.
  • To fund the game: ads, and measuring which campaigns lead to installs.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our contract with you (running the game), our legitimate interests (gameplay statistics, crash reports, non-personalised ads) and your consent (personalised ads and tracking, which you can withdraw at any time). We do not sell your personal information.

6. Who we share it with

  • NUVIAD: ads and install attribution (section 4).
  • Google AdMob: ads, when enabled (section 4).
  • Sentry: crash reports, if enabled.
  • Apple and Google: app store services, purchases and SKAdNetwork.
  • Hosting: our game server runs on a server rented by TonTech from a hosting provider.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

7. Your choices

  • Tracking: decline the tracking prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ad consent: where the consent prompt is shown, you can change your choice in the game's settings.
  • Remove ads: an optional purchase removes ads that appear on their own.

8. How long we keep information

  • Gameplay statistics and league data: up to 24 months.
  • Server logs (which include IP addresses): 30 days.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.

Your puzzle progress is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

9. Children

Gardendoku has a 4+ age rating but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA or UK under the GDPR, or in California under the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights; we will answer within 30 days. Because the game has no accounts, please include the app version and platform so we can help. Your information may be processed outside your country, for example by our service providers in the United States, with appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email info@tontech.co.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date.
