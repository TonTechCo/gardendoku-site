Frequently asked questions

How do hints work?

Tap the hint button and the game finds the next logical step and explains the reasoning behind it, so you can learn the technique instead of just copying the answer. Hints are limited; you can get more through optional purchases or by watching a rewarded ad.

What is the daily set?

Every day you get five new puzzles, one each of Queens, Tents, Binairo, Hitori and Light Up. Finishing them keeps your streak going and plants your garden.

What is a streak freeze?

A streak freeze protects your streak if you miss a day, so one busy day does not undo your progress.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account, so use the same store account you bought with.

I bought Remove Ads. Why do I still see rewarded ads?

Remove Ads removes the ads that appear on their own. Rewarded ads are always optional and only play when you choose to watch one in exchange for something, such as an extra hint, so they stay available.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Puzzles can be solved without a connection. The weekly league needs an internet connection to update.
