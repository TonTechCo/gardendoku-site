Five puzzle families

🌸 Queens

One flower in every row, column and bed. Flowers never touch.

⛺ Tents

Pitch one tent next to each tree. Tents never touch. Counts on the edges must match.

☀🌙 Binairo

Fill with suns and moons: equal numbers in every line, never three in a row, no two lines alike.

🌱 Hitori

Shade seeds so no number repeats in a line. Shaded seeds never touch, and the garden stays connected.

💡 Light Up

Place lanterns so every path is lit and no lantern shines on another. Numbered walls say how many lanterns touch them.
